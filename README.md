# 🛒 E-Commerce Customer Behavior & Revenue Analytics Dashboard

## 🔍 Project Overview
This project analyzes an e-commerce dataset containing 3,293 transactions to uncover insights into customer behavior, revenue trends, product performance, and transaction patterns.  
The dashboard was built using Tableau to support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior and spending patterns  
- Identify revenue trends and anomalies  
- Evaluate product category performance  
- Understand customer segmentation and engagement  
- Generate actionable business insights  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Tableau (Dashboard & Data Visualization)  
- Excel / CSV (Data Preparation)  
- Data Analysis & KPI Design  
- Data Storytelling

## 📈 Key KPIs
- Total Revenue: 212,373  
- Total Orders: 3,293  
- Average Order Value (AOV): 64.49  
- High Value Customers: 1,556  
- Unique Customers: 3,293  

---

## 🔥 Key Insights
- Identified a significant revenue drop (~78%) on a specific day, indicating a potential system or operational issue  
- No repeat customers observed, highlighting a gap in customer retention strategies  
- Beauty and Fashion categories generate the highest revenue  
- High-value customers are distributed across all age groups → behavioral segmentation is more effective than demographics  
- Peak transaction activity occurs during Wednesday lunchtime and Monday evenings  

---

## 💡 Business Impact
This analysis helps in:
- Identifying opportunities to improve customer retention  
- Optimizing marketing campaigns based on customer behavior  
- Focusing on high-performing product categories  
- Scheduling promotions during peak activity periods  
- Detecting and resolving operational issues affecting revenue
